Gumroad Fees: 10% If You Bring the Buyer, 30% If Gumroad Does

Gumroad charges triple when a customer finds you through its marketplace instead of your own link — and since 2025 it handles worldwide sales tax as Merchant of Record, which most comparisons ignore.

Skill level:BeginnerSetup time:An afternoon to list a productTime to first dollar:As soon as one person buysEarnings potential:70% – 89% of each sale

Prerequisites

  • A digital product people would pay for
  • Somewhere to send buyers from — a site, list, or social account

Gumroad prices the same way Udemy does, and for the same reason: it charges according to who found the customer.

The Two Rates

How the sale happened Gumroad takes
Your own profile or a direct link you shared 10% + $0.50
A new customer finding you through Discover, Gumroad's marketplace 30%

Gumroad describes the second as applying "when new customers find and buy from you through our discover marketplace."

There is no monthly subscription. Gumroad states its pricing has "no hidden fees, no monthly charges" — you pay only when you sell.

ℹ️ Good to Know

That structure tells you what Gumroad thinks it's selling. At 10% it is a checkout and delivery system. At 30% it is a storefront doing your marketing. Both are defensible prices for genuinely different services.

What You Keep

On a direct sale, the $0.50 matters more than it looks on small products:

Price Fees You keep Effective rate
$5 $1.00 $4.00 20.0%
$10 $1.50 $8.50 15.0%
$20 $2.50 $17.50 12.5%
$50 $5.50 $44.50 11.0%

Compare the same products sold through Discover at a flat 30%:

Price Direct (you keep) Discover (you keep) Difference
$10 $8.50 $7.00 $1.50
$20 $17.50 $14.00 $3.50
$50 $44.50 $35.00 $9.50

💡 Pro Tip

The gap widens with price, so if you sell higher-ticket products the case for driving your own traffic gets stronger fast. On a $50 product, every Discover sale costs you $9.50 relative to a direct one — which is a meaningful marketing budget you could spend instead.

The Part Most Comparisons Miss

Since 1 January 2025, Gumroad acts as Merchant of Record, and states it "handles ALL your tax obligations" including managing "sales tax collection and remittance worldwide."

This is easy to skim past and genuinely valuable. Selling digital products internationally means facing EU VAT, UK VAT, and a patchwork of US state sales tax rules — obligations that can apply from your first sale, with no minimum threshold in some jurisdictions.

As Merchant of Record, Gumroad is the seller of record for tax purposes and takes that on.

⚠️ Warning

This changes fee comparisons materially. A platform charging 5% where you handle your own global VAT registration and filing is not obviously cheaper than one charging 10% that handles it for you. Compliance has a real cost, whether you pay it in accountant fees or your own time — and getting it wrong has a cost too.

Note this covers sales tax on transactions. Your own income tax on the money you receive is still yours to deal with.

How It Compares

The pattern here is now familiar across creator platforms:

Platform You brought the buyer Platform brought the buyer
Gumroad 10% + $0.50 30%
Udemy 3% (you keep 97%) 63% (you keep 37%)

Udemy's spread is far more extreme, but the principle is identical — and so is the strategic conclusion. The audience you own is the asset. Every platform in this category prices access to its audience at a steep premium, because that's the expensive thing to build.

Gumroad's version is comparatively gentle. Even at its worst rate you keep 70%, where Udemy's marketplace rate leaves you 37%.

When Gumroad Makes Sense

It fits well if you already have somewhere to send people — a newsletter, a following, a site — and want checkout, file delivery and tax compliance without building any of it. At 10% + $0.50, that's cheap infrastructure.

It fits less well as a discovery engine. Discover exists and does bring buyers, but at 30% it should be a bonus rather than your plan.

Watch small-ticket pricing. At $5 a product you're surrendering 20% to the fixed fee alone. Bundling several small items into one higher-priced product improves your effective rate meaningfully.

✅ Action Step

Look at your last month of Gumroad sales and split them by Discover versus direct. If a large share came through Discover, the fastest available margin improvement is not raising prices — it's getting more of those same buyers to arrive through your own link.

Where to Verify Any of This

Both rates and the Merchant of Record arrangement come from Gumroad's own pricing page, linked below. Platform pricing changes, so confirm current rates there rather than relying on any summary, including this one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Gumroad take?

Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50 per transaction for sales through your own profile or direct links, and 30% per transaction when a new customer finds you through its Discover marketplace. There are no monthly charges.

Is Gumroad free to use?

There is no monthly subscription. Gumroad states its pricing is per transaction with no hidden fees and no monthly charges, so costs only arise when you actually sell something.

Does Gumroad handle sales tax and VAT?

Yes. Gumroad states that since 1 January 2025 it acts as Merchant of Record and handles all tax obligations, managing sales tax collection and remittance worldwide.

Why does Gumroad charge 30% sometimes?

The 30% rate applies when a new customer discovers and buys from you through Gumroad's Discover marketplace — meaning Gumroad found the buyer rather than you. Sales you drive to your own link stay at 10% + $0.50.

Amazon Associates Commission Rates: What Each Category Actually Pays

Amazon's affiliate rates run from 10% down to 0% depending entirely on category — and the products people assume are lucrative are often the worst paying. Here's the full rate card.

Skill level:BeginnerSetup time:An afternoon to pick a nicheTime to first dollar:Requires 3 qualifying sales in 180 daysEarnings potential:1% – 10% per qualifying sale

Prerequisites

  • A website, app, or social channel with genuine content
  • Willingness to disclose affiliate links clearly

Most affiliate advice treats Amazon as one rate. It isn't. Amazon pays a fixed percentage that depends entirely on product category, and the spread runs from 10% down to nothing at all.

Picking your niche without reading the rate card is the single most expensive mistake in Amazon affiliate marketing, because you can build genuine traffic around products that pay almost nothing.

The Rate Card

Rate Categories
10% Luxury Beauty, Luxury Stores Beauty, Amazon Explore
5% Digital Music, Physical Music, Handmade, Digital Videos
4.5% Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive
4% Tablets, Kindles, smartwatches, Fire TV, Echo devices — and All Other Categories
3% Toys, furniture, home goods, sports, baby products, musical instruments
2.5% PC products, DVDs
2% Video games, televisions
1% Groceries, video game consoles, health items
0% Gift cards, wireless plans, alcohol, restaurant food delivery, Coach products

⚠️ Warning

Look at the bottom of that table before you pick a niche. A site reviewing televisions earns 2%, and one covering video game consoles earns 1% — on products costing hundreds of dollars, with buyers who research heavily and compare prices. Meanwhile a site about kitchen equipment earns 4.5% on far cheaper items that people buy on impulse.

Price Times Rate Is the Only Number That Matters

High-ticket does not mean high-paying. Work it through:

Product Price Rate Commission
Games console $500 1% $5.00
Television $800 2% $16.00
Kitchen mixer $350 4.5% $15.75
Luxury beauty set $150 10% $15.00

A $150 beauty product pays roughly the same as an $800 television. The television requires far more persuasion, competes against every major retailer, and attracts price-comparison shoppers who may well buy elsewhere.

ℹ️ Good to Know

This is why so many successful Amazon affiliate sites cover kitchen equipment, home goods, books and beauty rather than electronics. It isn't that those writers prefer the topics — it's that the rate card makes the arithmetic work.

Bounties Are Separate

Alongside percentage commissions, Amazon pays flat bounty payments for specific sign-up actions — for example $3 for a Prime sign-up, and $5–$25 for Audible memberships.

Bounties are worth understanding because they don't depend on order value. A single Prime sign-up can outpay several low-rate product sales, which is why service and sign-up recommendations sit alongside product reviews on established affiliate sites.

The Rules That Get Accounts Closed

Amazon's Associates Program has unusually specific requirements, and violations end the account rather than producing a warning:

You must disclose the relationship. This is both an Amazon requirement and an FTC one. The FTC's endorsement guides require disclosures to be clear and conspicuous — meaning visible before the link, not buried in a footer or hidden behind "read more".

You cannot use affiliate links in email or ebooks. Links must live on your approved sites.

You cannot obscure the destination with link shorteners that hide it, or otherwise mislead about where a click leads.

You must make qualifying sales to stay in the programme. New accounts need to generate qualifying sales within a set window or they're closed — Amazon states the requirement as three qualifying sales within 180 days of applying.

Prices go stale. Amazon's terms restrict displaying prices you've hardcoded, because they change constantly. Use Amazon's own tools rather than typing prices into your article.

⚠️ Warning

The disclosure requirement is not optional and not a formality. It is enforced by both Amazon and, separately, the FTC. A clear line such as "We earn a commission from purchases made through links on this page" placed above the first link satisfies both — and costs you nothing in conversions, because readers already assume it.

What This Means for Content Strategy

Google's spam policies name thin affiliate pages — pages that add nothing to what the merchant already says — as a violation. So the strategy that works is the same one that works everywhere else: cover products you actually understand, add information a reader can't get from the product listing, and be honest when something isn't worth buying.

The rate card should inform which niche you go deep on. It should not turn your site into a link farm for whatever pays most.

✅ Action Step

Before committing to a niche, multiply a typical product price in that category by its commission rate, then ask how many of those sales a month you'd need for the income you want. Do it for two or three candidate niches. The results usually redirect people away from expensive electronics and toward mid-priced goods with a 4.5% or higher rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Amazon affiliate commission rate?

It depends entirely on category. Amazon's published rates run from 10% for Luxury Beauty and Amazon Explore down to 1% for groceries, video game consoles and health items, with several categories at 0%. The default for "All Other Categories" is 4%.

Which Amazon categories pay affiliates the most?

Luxury Beauty, Luxury Stores Beauty and Amazon Explore pay 10%. Digital and physical music, Handmade and digital videos pay 5%. Physical books, kitchen and automotive pay 4.5%.

Which Amazon products pay no commission?

Amazon lists gift cards, wireless plans, alcohol, restaurant food delivery and Coach brand products at 0%. Promoting those earns nothing regardless of how many you sell.

Do electronics pay well on Amazon Associates?

Generally no, which surprises people. Televisions and video games pay 2%, PC products and DVDs 2.5%, and video game consoles just 1%. High-ticket electronics are among the worst-paying categories by percentage.
